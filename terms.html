What Deal Guard is, and isn't

Deal Guard helps you research a used vehicle and organize your car-buying decision. It provides information and estimates, including data from NHTSA and the EPA, a computed Reliability Signal, market price comparisons, cost estimates, and AI-generated suggestions.

Deal Guard's standard VIN reports are not a vehicle history report. Apart from a check for salvage auction sale records, they do not report accidents, title brands, flood history, odometer records, or liens, and they are not a substitute for a vehicle history report (such as Carfax, AutoCheck, or an NMVTIS report). Where offered, the optional full history report is a one-time purchase adding a vehicle history report for a single VIN from a third-party vehicle history provider (a CARFAX or AutoCheck report where one exists for that VIN, otherwise an NMVTIS report). A CARFAX or AutoCheck report may be a copy obtained earlier for the same VIN, up to a year old, when the provider shows no new records for the car since; the app shows the date it was run. Its contents come from records we do not control and may be incomplete or delayed. A vehicle history report is NOT a substitute for an independent vehicle inspection. Deal Guard is not a professional inspection, a valuation, financial or legal advice, or a guarantee about any vehicle. Always verify a vehicle in person and consider an independent mechanic's inspection before purchase.

Estimates and third-party data

Reliability Signals, market comparisons, tax/fee/loan/ownership estimates, and AI outputs are approximations that can be wrong or incomplete. Listing data comes from third-party sources we don't control and may be out of date, inaccurate, or removed. You are responsible for your own purchase decisions.

Subscriptions and purchases

Deal Guard offers auto-renewing subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Any free-trial period ends if you subscribe, and unused trial time is forfeited when you buy a subscription. The free trial does not include salvage auction records.

The Shopping Pass is a one-time purchase that does not renew. It unlocks the Nitro plan's features for 30 days from purchase (a pass bought while another is running adds 30 days after it ends), then simply ends. It does not include history reports. It's restored with Restore Purchases on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Where offered, the full history report (a third-party vehicle history report) is a one-time purchase that grants one report credit. A credit is consumed when you redeem it for a specific VIN; the fulfilled report is then permanently tied to that VIN and stays readable in the app at no further charge.

Where offered, salvage auction records (Copart and IAAI sale details and photos) open with a paid Nitro plan, a Shopping Pass, or a history report bought for that car. Each Nitro billing period and each Shopping Pass includes a limited number of new auction records; records you already opened stay free. With a new Shopping Pass, new auction records open 48 hours after purchase (right away with a history report for that car). Auction records and photos come from the auctions and our provider, which we do not control. They show the car as it was at the sale and may be incomplete.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's policies. When Apple refunds a purchase, what it paid for (a report credit, a Shopping Pass, or a plan's included reports and auction records) stops working.
