Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

Deal Guard is built to help you vet a used car before you buy it. We designed it to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what that means.

The short version

  • Deal Guard has no account and no login.
  • Your deals, saved searches, photos, notes, and reports are stored on your device (and, if you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud account — never ours).
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • A few requests go through our own server so the app doesn't have to carry the keys for paid data services. It keeps very little (see below).

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — tracked deals, saved searches, buyer checklists, photos, notes, and cached vehicle reports — is stored locally using Apple's on-device database. If you turn on iCloud sync, that data syncs through your personal iCloud account so it survives a new phone; it is end-to-end within your Apple account and is not accessible to us.

What we send to third parties, and why

To produce a report or listings, the app sends the minimum needed to these services, directly from your device unless noted:

  • NHTSA and the EPA (U.S. government) — the VIN or year/make/model to look up recalls, complaints, crash-test ratings, and fuel economy.
  • Vehicle-listing sources (e.g. MarketCheck, eBay, and public marketplace pages) — your search terms and ZIP to find matching listings. In current versions of the app, Facebook Marketplace searches go through our server, which sends your search terms and the approximate map location of your ZIP.
  • Anthropic — only for message drafts and the listing chat, when those are available: the vehicle details shown in that feature are sent through our server to Anthropic's API to write the response. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. Deal evaluations and the contract Fee Scan run entirely on your iPhone and send nothing.
  • VinAudit (an approved NMVTIS data provider) — only if you purchase a Premium VIN report and redeem it, the VIN you chose is sent to generate that report, through our server. Nothing is sent by merely buying a credit, and never for VINs you don't redeem it on.

We do not attach your name or an account to these requests, because there isn't one.

Our server

Deal Guard runs a small server (dealguard-api.entitysolutions.co, operated by Entity Solutions LLC) that passes Premium VIN report and Facebook Marketplace requests to those services, so their keys never ship inside the app. To make sure a request really comes from the Deal Guard app, your device uses Apple's App Attest, which creates a key that stays on your device; our server keeps only its public half. What the server keeps:

  • For each Premium VIN report purchase, Apple's purchase ID and the VIN it was used on, so one purchase can't be used twice. Apple's purchase record does not include your name, Apple ID, or payment details.
  • Daily request counts per device and per IP address, used only to stop abuse, deleted after 30 days.

It does not keep your search terms, your location, or the reports themselves.

Camera, location, and notifications

  • Camera is used to scan a VIN and, if you use the Fee Scan, to photograph the dealer's contract. Both are read on your device. The images are never uploaded, and the contract photo is not saved; only the fee breakdown is.
  • Location / ZIP is used only to find local listings and estimate taxes and fees.
  • Notifications, if you allow them, are used to alert you to new matching listings and price drops.

Payments

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in Settings → "Delete all local data." That clears the on-device database, cached reports, and app settings. If iCloud sync is on, deleting on one device removes it from your other devices too.

Children

Deal Guard is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We'll update this policy as the app changes and revise the date above.
