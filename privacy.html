Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

Deal Guard is built to help you vet a used car before you buy it. We \

designed it to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what \

that means.

The short version

  • Deal Guard has no account and no login.
  • Your deals, saved searches, photos, notes, and reports are stored on \

your device (and, if you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud \

account — never ours).

  • We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or \

analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — tracked deals, saved searches, buyer \

checklists, photos, notes, and cached vehicle reports — is stored \

locally using Apple's on-device database. If you turn on iCloud sync, \

that data syncs through your personal iCloud account so it survives a \

new phone; it is end-to-end within your Apple account and is not \

accessible to us.

What we send to third parties, and why

To produce a report or listings, the app sends the minimum needed \

directly from your device to these services:

  • NHTSA and the EPA (U.S. government) — the VIN or year/make/model \

to look up recalls, complaints, crash-test ratings, and fuel economy.

  • Vehicle-listing sources (e.g. MarketCheck, eBay, and public \

marketplace pages) — your search terms and ZIP to find matching \

listings.

  • Anthropic — if you use an AI feature (deal evaluation, the search \

concierge, or message drafts), the relevant vehicle details are sent to \

Anthropic's API to generate that result. This data is not used to train \

models.

We do not attach your name or an account to these requests, because \

there isn't one.

Camera, location, and notifications

  • Camera is used only to scan a VIN. Scanning happens on your \

device; images are not uploaded.

  • Location / ZIP is used only to find local listings and estimate \

taxes and fees.

  • Notifications, if you allow them, are used to alert you to new \

matching listings and price drops.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see \

your card or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in Settings → "Delete all local \

data." That clears the on-device database, cached reports, and app \

settings. If iCloud sync is on, deleting on one device removes it from \

your other devices too.

Children

Deal Guard is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly \

collect data from them.

Changes

We'll update this policy as the app changes and revise the date above.
